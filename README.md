🍕 Pizza Sales Analytics Dashboard | Power BI Project
Excited to share my Pizza Sales Analytics Dashboard, created to transform sales data into clear and actionable business insights using Power BI.
This dashboard focuses on understanding sales performance, order trends, customer ordering patterns, and product preferences.
📊 Key KPIs
Total Revenue: 817.86K
Average Order Value: 38.31
Total Pizzas Sold: 49.57K
Total Orders: 21K
Average Pizzas per Order: 2.32
🔍 Key Insights
Orders show stronger activity toward the end of the week, particularly Friday and Saturday.
July records the highest monthly order volume in the dashboard.
Classic Pizza contributes the largest share of sales by category.
Large-size pizzas represent the highest share of sales by pizza size.
The dashboard helps compare monthly trends, pizza categories, sizes, and total orders in one place.
